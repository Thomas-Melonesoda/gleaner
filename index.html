Gleaner拾穗

把和 AI 的对话收回来，
拾起值得留下的部分，
变成你自己的笔记。 Bring your AI conversations home. Glean what is worth keeping, and make it your own notes.

名字来自米勒的《拾穗者》：收割过后，田里还留着麦穗，拾穗的人弯下腰一穗一穗拾起来。和 AI 的对话也是一片收割过的田。The name comes from Millet’s The Gleaners: after the harvest, grain is still left in the field, and the gleaners pick it up ear by ear. A conversation with AI is a harvested field too.

正午Noon

收进来，拾起来Bring it in, glean it

1

收进来源区Bring it into the source pane

把和 AI 的对话、读过的资料收进左栏，文字、公式、表格、代码、图片和附件原样保留。Bring conversations with AI and the material you read into the left pane, with text, formulas, tables, code, images and attachments intact.

  • 浏览器扩展：在 ChatGPT、Claude 网页上点一下，整段对话导入；可以只选其中几轮。Browser extension: one click on ChatGPT or Claude imports the whole conversation, or just the turns you pick.
  • Codex、Claude Code：直接读这台电脑上的会话；或者在对话里说"存到拾穗"。Codex and Claude Code: read sessions straight from this computer, or say “save to Gleaner” in the conversation.
  • PDF 和图片：拖进左栏。PDFs and images: drag them in.

每一轮都写明哪句是你问的、哪句是 AI 答的。Every turn shows what you asked and what the AI answered.

2

摘录到笔记Glean into your note

在左栏选中一句，点"拾进笔记"，原话就落进中间的笔记。摘录末尾有一个小麦穗，点它跳回原文那一句。摘录可以改写、调顺序；段落、摘录、表格、公式都能整块选中、上下移动、删除。Select a sentence on the left and click “Glean into note”: the words land in your note in the middle. A small grain at the end of each excerpt takes you back to the original. Rewrite and reorder excerpts freely; select any paragraph, excerpt, table or formula as a whole block to move or delete it.

午后Afternoon

写下想法，带走Write in the margin, take it with you

3

页边备注Comments in the margin

想法写在右边的页边，不打断笔记正文。选中笔记里的一句话加备注，它就挂在那句话上，点"定位到笔记"随时回去；也可以写一段给整篇笔记的备注。在左栏读到要紧的话，也能直接拾进备注。Write your thoughts in the margin on the right, without breaking up the note. Comment on a sentence and the comment stays attached to it; “Show in note” takes you back. Write a comment for the whole note too, or glean a passage from the left straight into a comment.

4

导出和打包Export and pack

导出成 PDF、Word 或 Markdown：备注变成文末注释或 Word 批注；摘录后带一个小编号，文末列出来源，能点回原对话。也可以把笔记、备注和用到的材料打包成一个学习笔记包，发给同学，对方用拾穗打开，内容一样，还能跳回原文。Export to PDF, Word or Markdown: comments become endnotes or Word comments, each excerpt gets a small number, and the sources are listed at the end with links back to the conversations. Or pack notes, comments and their material into one study pack; a classmate opens it in Gleaner and gets the same notes, still linked to the originals.

夜Night

开始用Get started

  1. 下载 AppDownload the app

    Apple 芯片（M 系列）的 Mac，macOS 13 或更新。打开 dmg，把拾穗拖进"应用程序"。Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later. Open the dmg and drag Gleaner into Applications.

  2. 浏览器扩展Browser extension

    从 Chrome 网上应用店或 Edge 加载项安装。第一次用时输入 App 右上角的配对码。Install it from the Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons. The first time, enter the pairing code shown at the top right of the app.

  3. Codex、Claude Code 插件Codex and Claude Code plugins

    在 App 里打开"导入 Codex / Claude Code 会话"，点"安装插件"。In the app, open “Import Codex / Claude Code sessions” and click “Install plugin”.

对话和笔记都只存在你自己的电脑上，不上传，不需要注册。Conversations and notes stay on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.
