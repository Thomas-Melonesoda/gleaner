正午Noon

收割过的田A harvested field

和 ChatGPT、Claude、Codex 的对话，原样收进来。读的时候，把值得留下的拾进自己的笔记。 Your conversations with ChatGPT, Claude and Codex come in word for word. As you read, glean what is worth keeping into notes of your own.

拾进笔记Glean into a note

左边是原来的对话，中间是你的笔记。选中一句拾进来，原句留一道麦色的光；拾过的地方，页边多一穗麦，随时跳回原文。 The conversation on the left, your note in the middle. Select a sentence and glean it; the original keeps a wheat-gold trace, and a small ear in the margin takes you back to it.

写在页边Write in the margin

三种字各有分工：AI 的原文用黑体，你的笔记用宋体，页边的备注用楷体。一眼就分得清哪些是拾来的，哪些是自己的。 Three voices, three typefaces: the AI’s text in sans, your notes in serif, your margin comments in a brush script. You always know what you gleaned and what you wrote.

带走Take it with you

导出成 PDF、Word 或 Markdown，三种字跟着走；也可以打包成一个学习笔记包，别人打开还能跳回原文。 Export to PDF, Word or Markdown with the three voices intact, or pack notes and sources into one study pack that still jumps back to the original.

午后Afternoon

为什么叫拾穗Why “Gleaner”

名字来自米勒的《拾穗者》。收割过后，田里还留着麦穗，拾穗的人弯下腰，一穗一穗拾起来，带回自己的地方。三十年后，梵高在圣雷米照着米勒的版画，又画了一遍田里劳作的人。 The name comes from Millet’s The Gleaners. After the harvest, grain is still left in the field; the gleaners bend down, pick it up ear by ear, and carry it home. Thirty years later, at Saint-Rémy, Van Gogh painted Millet’s field workers again from prints.

和 AI 的对话也是一片收割过的田：在这里拾起值得留下的，变成你自己的笔记。 A conversation with AI is a harvested field too: glean what is worth keeping, and make it your own.
